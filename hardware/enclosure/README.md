# Bisque Kiln Controller — Enclosure Design

A build-it-from-catalog-parts enclosure for the complete controller: the
rev B PCB, the 4.0" display, the 5-way nav switch, the 5 V supply, both
SSRs and their heatsinks, the mains distribution, and the CT sensors. The
enclosure itself is an **off-the-shelf wall-mount steel box** — no custom
sheet-metal fabrication, only drilling and one rectangular cutout (the
display window), all of which is covered by the printable 1:1 template
(`door-template.svg`, regenerated by `generate_panel_template.py`).

> **Safety scope.** Everything mains-side in this document must follow
> local electrical code, and none of it replaces the mechanical
> protections `hardware/kicad/README.md` requires: a mechanical
> over-temperature cutout and/or contactor in series with the elements,
> appropriately rated branch-circuit protection, and supervision while
> firing. The on-board watchdog and lid interlock are supplementary.

## What goes in the box

| Item | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bisque controller PCB | `hardware/kicad/` | 100 × 100 mm, M3 holes on a 90 × 90 mm grid, **grounded** — metal standoffs deliberately bond board GND to the earthed door |
| Display module | LCDWIKI **MSP4021** (4.0" ST7796S SPI) | PCB 61.74 × 108.04 mm, active area 55.68 × 83.52 mm, used landscape; 14-pin loom to J5 |
| Nav switch | 5 × 12 mm panel-mount momentary pushbuttons | Cross layout (Up/Down/Left/Right/Select), wired to J6; active-low to GND, no resistors needed (ESP32 pull-ups) |
| 5 V supply | Mean Well **HDR-15-5** (5 V / 2.4 A, DIN rail) | Board + display + backlight + buzzer + SSR inputs peak well under 1.5 A — ~1.6× headroom minimum |
| Aux coil supply (optional) | Mean Well HDR-15-12 or -24 | Only if a vent/purge solenoid hangs off J10; feeds `AUX_VP`. Leave `SJ1` open when fitted |
| SSR × 2 | Omron **G3NA-240B** (40 A) or Crydom D2440-class | DC input 5–24 V, zero-cross, **with a datasheet control↔load isolation rating** — this is the only mains barrier to the board, see the KiCad README |

## Enclosure selection

**Baseline: a generic imported 16 × 12 × 6 in (406 × 305 × 152 mm) IP66
carbon-steel wall-mount box** — hinged, latched door, 1.5 mm
cold-rolled steel, sold **with a galvanized inner mounting plate
included** — the Vevor/SKYSHALO-class boxes on Amazon/Walmart, ~$60.
The name-brand equivalent (Hammond **EN4SD16126GY** + **EP1612** panel,
Saginaw/Hoffman similar) is the same box mechanically but runs
**~$240 + ~$35 for the panel** — worth it only if you need the **UL 50/508A
listing** for an inspected or commercial installation, or a
NEMA 4/12 rating you'd actually preserve (the baseline design vents the
box for the SSR chimney anyway, which forfeits the sealed rating
regardless of what you paid for it).

Two things to check on an import that a Hammond buyer gets for free:
that the **door is bonded** to the body (most include a ground screw on
door and body — fit a braided strap between them if there's no factory
strap), and that the inner plate is actually steel and lands on welded
studs, not adhesive standoffs.

Why this class of box:

- **The door is the front panel.** 305 × 406 mm of flat steel takes the
  display window, the nav cross, and the controller PCB with room to
  spare, and hinges open for USB-C access without unwiring anything.
- **Depth.** 152 mm clears an SSR + internal heatsink stack (~90 mm off
  the back panel) plus the door-mounted PCB and looms (~30 mm) with
  ~30 mm to spare.
- **Steel = shield.** The board already assumes a grounded steel shell
  (U.FL external antenna, grounded mounting holes); a plastic box would
  waste both decisions.
- **The inner panel** carries all the mains-side gear, so the box shell
  itself gets only holes, and the whole mains assembly can be built and
  wired on the bench.

Sizing alternatives, same series so every dimension below scales
trivially:

| Situation | Box |
|---|---|
| ≤ 25 A per zone, no contactor | 16 × 12 × 6 in generic (baseline), ~$60 |
| Up to 40 A per zone, contactor + aux PSU fitted | 20 × 16 × 6 in generic (~$80–100); Hammond EN4SD20166GY + EP2016 if listed gear is required |
| Inspected / commercial install | Hammond EN4SD16126GY + EP1612 (UL listed, ~$275 all-in) |

## Layout

Two zones, hard-segregated: **everything SELV lives on the door,
everything mains lives on the back panel.** The only things that cross
are the SSR control pairs, the CT twisted pairs, the 5 V feed, and (if
fitted) the aux coil feed — all SELV, all in one loomed bundle across
the hinge with a strain-relieved slack loop.

```
        DOOR (inside view)                 BACK PANEL (inner plate)
  ┌──────────────────────────┐         ┌───────────────────────────────┐
  │      ┌────────────┐      │         │  DIN RAIL:                    │
  │      │  display   │      │         │  [PE][IN][FUSE][HDR-15-5][AUX]│
  │      │  window    │      │         │                               │
  │      └────────────┘      │         │  ┌────────┐    ┌────────┐    │
  │           (U)            │         │  │ SSR 1  │    │ SSR 2  │    │
  │        (L)(S)(R)         │         │  │  on    │    │  on    │    │
  │           (D)            │         │  │heatsink│    │heatsink│    │
  │      ┌────────────┐      │         │  └────────┘    └────────┘    │
  │      │ controller │      │         │   CT1 ○         CT2 ○        │
  │      │    PCB     │      │         │                               │
  │      └────────────┘      │         │  [load terminal blocks]       │
  └──────────────────────────┘         └───────────────────────────────┘
       hinge side: SELV loom              bottom: cable glands
```

Board orientation on the door: rotate the PCB so its **south edge
(J5/J6/J7/J11) faces up**, toward the display — the 14-pin display loom
and the nav loom stay short (SPI runs at 40 MHz; keep the display loom
under ~150 mm). That puts USB-C (J1) at the bottom, reachable with the
door open, the SSR/power terminals (J2/J10/J4/J9) on one side facing the
hinge loom, and the TC/CT terminals (J3/J8/J12) on the other.

**Thermocouple routing.** J3/J8's screw terminals are the cold junction
reference — the MAX31856s compensate on-die a few mm away. Door mounting
is what keeps them away from the SSR heat plume; run the K-type
extension wire up the SELV side, ≥ 50 mm from any mains conductor, with
no intermediate junctions between the kiln probe and the board terminal.
Both probes must be **ungrounded-junction** (see the KiCad README).

**Antenna.** SMA bulkhead through the enclosure's top-left side wall
(SELV side), 6.5 mm D-hole. The U.FL end is good for ~30 mating cycles —
connect it once, at final assembly, and route the pigtail away from the
SSR wiring. Stay within the module's certified antenna list (CERT-001 in
`FAB-READINESS-REVIEW-REVB.md`).

### Door drilling (see `door-template.svg`, print 1:1)

Coordinates from the door's top-left corner, door mounted 406 mm tall.
All positions are centered horizontally at x = 152.5 mm.

| Feature | Position (center) | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Display window | (152.5, 110) | 86 × 58 mm rectangular cutout |
| Display module M3 holes | corners of the module PCB, nominally inset 2.5 mm | ⌀3.2 — **measure your module before drilling**; clone modules vary, and the template marks these as VERIFY |
| Nav buttons ×5 | cross at (152.5, 190), 24 mm pitch | ⌀12.2 each |
| Controller PCB M3 holes | (152.5 ± 45, 290 ± 45) | ⌀3.2, the board's 90 × 90 mm grid |

The window is cut 86 × 58 so it reveals the full 83.5 × 55.7 mm active
area with ~1 mm margin while staying inside the module's glass. Bond a
gasketed 2 mm polycarbonate window behind the cutout — kiln rooms are
dusty, and nothing needs the resistive touch surface exposed (firmware
has no touch driver; the nav switch is the input). Mount the module on
M3 standoffs so the glass sits just behind the window; mount the PCB on
**metal** M3 standoffs — H1–H4 are grounded on purpose, and the standoffs
bond board GND to the earthed door.

### Back panel and shell

- **DIN rail** across the top of the inner plate: PE terminal, incoming line
  terminals, a fuse holder (1 A slow-blow) feeding the HDR-15-5, an
  optional second fused position for the aux PSU. Feed the controller
  from its own small circuit or tap ahead of the SSRs per local code —
  never downstream of them.
- **SSRs** on the lower half, one per zone, fins vertical, ≥ 25 mm apart
  and ≥ 25 mm from the panel edges. Load-side wiring in the bottom-right
  quadrant only.
- **CTs** clamp each zone's load conductor right at the SSR output
  terminal block; twist each secondary pair to J12.
- **Cable glands** through the bottom wall (never the top of a NEMA
  box): mains in (M25), zone 1 out (M25), zone 2 out (M25), TC1 (M16),
  TC2 (M16), spare M16 for lid switch / aux / inputs.
- **Grounding:** enclosure PE stud → incoming earth; door bonded to the
  body with a braided strap (factory-fitted on name-brand boxes; add one
  if the import lacks it); inner plate bonded via its mounting studs and
  ground screw; every gland on the
  mains side metallic and seated on bare metal or star washers.

## Thermal design

The SSRs are the only significant heat source. A hockey-puck SSR drops
roughly 1.0–1.6 V, so budget **≈ 1.25 W per amp per zone**:

| Per-zone element current | Dissipation per SSR | Heatsink + airflow |
|---|---|---|
| ≤ 12 A | ~15 W | Y92B-N100-class (≤ 1.7 K/W), passive |
| 12–25 A | ~30 W | ≤ 1.0 K/W or vendor-paired sink, louvred chimney (below) |
| 25–40 A | ~50 W | Vendor-paired sink **plus** a 60 mm fan on the chimney; or a through-wall heatsink kit that puts the fins outside |

The sizing rule behind that table: keep the SSR base ≤ 80 °C at your
worst-case in-box ambient (design for 50 °C inside — a kiln room runs
hot), so `R_sink ≤ (80 − 50) / (1.25 × I_zone)`. Whatever sink you fit,
verify the base temperature with a thermocouple during the first
full-power firing; if it exceeds 80 °C, add the fan. Always follow the
SSR vendor's own derating curve for the specific SSR/heatsink pairing
(Omron publishes G3NA ↔ Y92B charts), and derate the SSR itself to
≤ 70 % of nameplate.

**Ventilation.** For the passive chimney: a louvred vent low on the
right (mains) side wall and another high on the same wall, both with
insect mesh, giving convection a straight path up the heatsink fins.
This drops the box from NEMA 4/12 to effectively NEMA 1 — fine for an
indoor kiln room; if the box lives somewhere wet or dusty, use the
through-wall heatsink option and keep the shell sealed. The door-mounted
electronics benefit from the same segregation: the PCB and display hang
in the cool front half of the box, out of the heatsink plume, which also
keeps the cold-junction terminals stable.

The HDR-15-5 adds ~2 W; the on-board LDO's headroom analysis in the KiCad
README already assumes a hot enclosure ambient, so no further derating is
needed.

**The 5 V rail is now a hard 5 V, not "5-ish".** U2 is a TLV1117LV33,
whose absolute-maximum input is **6 V** (the AMS1117 it replaced tolerated
15 V). Verify the PSU's output before connecting the board — the HDR-15-5's
trim pot has roughly +/-10% travel and ships adjustable — and never
substitute a 12 V supply for the board feed. A 12 V aux supply for J10 is
fine; it reaches only `AUX_VP`, never the board rail, provided `SJ1` stays
open.

## Commissioning notes

1. **First power-on with unflashed firmware: both SSR channels are dead
   by design.** The hardware watchdog holds `SSR_EN` down until firmware
   kicks GPIO 36. Before concluding an SSR or wiring fault, read the
   `SJ2` / "WDT DEFEAT" section of `hardware/kicad/jlcpcb/README.md`.
   **Leave `SJ2` open in a finished install** — bridging it defeats the
   only heat cutoff that survives firmware death. It is a bench-debug
   jumper, not a build step.
2. **No lid switch fitted?** Jumper J11 pin 1 to J11 GND or set
   `KILN_PIN_LID_SWITCH=-1`, or the kiln will never heat — see
   `docs/pin-assignments.md` §1.
3. Verify the SSR control polarity: J4/J9 pin 1 is `SSR_EN` (+5 V,
   watchdog-gated) to the SSR's `+` input, pin 2 is the switched low
   side to `−`. The amber board LEDs show true drive state.
4. Megger/continuity-check the mains side with the SELV loom
   disconnected, prove the over-temp cutout drops the contactor, then
   fire the bench smoke test (`docs/bench-smoke-test.md`) before the
   first real firing.

## Regenerating the template

```bash
python3 hardware/enclosure/generate_panel_template.py
```

Stdlib-only; rewrites `door-template.svg` in place. All dimensions are
parameters at the top of the script — adjust `DISPLAY_HOLE_*` after
measuring your actual display module, re-run, and print at 100 % scale
(the template carries a 100 mm calibration ruler to check the printer
didn't scale it).
