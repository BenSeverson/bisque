### Failures

**1. No stackup (blocks impedance sign-off).** `board_info.stackup` is empty, so `check_impedance.py` omitted every Z0 number. USB_DP/DN are routed at 0.25–0.3 mm width against a netclass `diff_pair_width` of 0.2 / `diff_pair_gap` 0.25 — nothing on this board ties that geometry to USB's 90 Ω differential target.

**2. USB_DP / USB_DN — the substantive routing defect.** Both are multipoint (J1 receptacle → U4 → U1.14/13), so total copper is the wrong measure; these are pin-pair path lengths:

| Leg | DP | DN | Skew |
|---|---|---|---|
| J1.B6/B7 → U4 | 8.366 mm | 6.595 mm | 1.77 mm |
| U4 → U1 (MCU) | 26.298 mm | 27.776 mm | 1.48 mm |
| End-to-end J1 → U1 | 34.801 mm | 31.412 mm | **3.39 mm** |

That is ~7× the 0.5 mm review threshold and well past USB 2.0 HS intra-pair guidance (0.15 mm strict, ~1.25 mm relaxed). Coupling is also inconsistent: only 16.1 mm of DP runs within 1 mm of DN, and the edge-to-edge gap wanders 0.250 → 0.995 mm (median 0.584) against a 0.25 mm design gap. Via counts are asymmetric (6 on DP, 5 on DN), and 19% of each net's length (7.67 / 7.20 mm) runs over a void in the reference plane.

**3. GND return via coverage — 55 of 68 signal vias uncovered.** Worst offenders: I2C_SDA 14.55 mm, LED_DATA 14.35 mm, SPI_SCLK 12.58 mm, SPI_MISO 11.85 mm. USB is included: the via at (63.50, 40.75) has its nearest GND return 7.07 mm away against a 2.0 mm target. 76 GND vias exist board-wide (134 return points including through-hole GND pads), but they are not placed near the signal transitions.

**4. Plane architecture compounds #3.** In1.Cu is GND, In2.Cu is +3V3, and *all 370 vias are F.Cu→B.Cu through vias*. Every layer change therefore swaps the reference from GND to +3V3, so a plain GND stitching via does not close the return loop at those points — they need a GND↔3V3 stitching capacitor beside the transition. B.Cu traces reference the +3V3 pour, not ground.
